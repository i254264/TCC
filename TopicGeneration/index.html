What is
Topic Modeling?

Overview

Nowadays, vast amounts of literature across academic, corporate, and social fields are digitized and stored electronically. Topic modeling provides unsupervised statistical algorithms that automatically reveal, discover, and organize latent thematic structures in large document collections without requiring manual human labeling or annotation.

This platform is powered by Gensim, a specialized Python library engineered for scalable natural language processing and semantic modeling. To understand how unstructured text is transformed into meaningful topics, Gensim structures its entire workflow around four core concepts: Documents, Corpora, Vectors, and Models.

Gensim Core Concepts
Document: Any text sequence (e.g., an article title or abstract).
Corpus: A collection of documents used as input to train unsupervised models.
Vector: Mathematical representation of text in a numerical vector space (e.g., Bag-of-Words).
Model: Gensim algorithms transforming document vectors to discover latent themes.

In summary: Raw documents are assembled into a corpus, mapped into mathematical vectors, and then transformed by models (like LDA, LSA, and Word2Vec) to reveal latent topics, highlight word associations, and reduce statistical noise across large corpora.
