User Guide

Welcome to Topic Modeling. Start testing topic models now by uploading your files.

Topic modeling is a type of statistical model for discovering the abstract "topics" that occur in a collection of documents. It can be used to analyze large volumes of text data and identify patterns and relationships between words and topics.

In this application, you can upload your own files to generate topics based on the content of those files. The application will process the files and provide you with a list of topics that are present in the documents.
