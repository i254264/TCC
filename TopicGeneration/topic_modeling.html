Modeling Guide

A beginner-friendly guide to choosing a model, setting options, and understanding results.

What is it?

LSA reads all your documents to find words that frequently appear together. It connects these words to form hidden themes, helping you discover the main subjects covered in your files. It achieves this through matrix factorization (SVD) to identify latent patterns.

Parameters

  • topics: How many different themes or subjects you want to discover.
  • words: How many keywords to show inside each theme.
  • interaction: How many calculation passes the model makes (higher values are slower but can be more precise).
  • clean corpus: Removes common words like "the", "at", "is", as well as numbers and special characters.
  • lemmatize corpus: Simplifies word endings to their root grammatical form (e.g., converts "running" to "run").

Outputs & Visualization

Interactive cards are generated below, each representing a topic group led by its main term (Group Keyword: "...").

  • Numerical Value: Strength Score — Measures how strongly this word is tied to the topic. Higher scores mean the word plays a bigger role in defining that theme.
  • Percentage (%): Relative Relevance — Compares each word's importance against the group keyword (100%). A 50% score means the term is half as strong as the leader.
  • Spreadsheet Export: Download containing the complete breakdown of topics and word scores.

What is it?

LDA reads all your documents to find words that frequently appear together. It connects these words to form hidden themes, helping you discover the main subjects covered in your files. Unlike LSA, it uses a probabilistic model, assuming documents are mixtures of multiple topics.

Parameters

  • topics: How many different themes or subjects you want to discover.
  • words: How many keywords to show inside each theme.
  • interaction: How many calculation passes the model makes (higher values are slower but can be more precise).
  • clean corpus: Removes common words like "the", "at", "is", as well as numbers and special characters.
  • lemmatize corpus: Simplifies word endings to their root grammatical form (e.g., converts "running" to "run").

Outputs & Visualization

Interactive cards are generated below, each representing a topic group led by its main term (Group Keyword: "...").

  • Numerical Value: Topic Probability — The mathematical chance of this word appearing whenever this topic is discussed across your documents.
  • Percentage (%): Relative Frequency — Shows how often this word occurs compared to the group keyword (100%).
  • Spreadsheet Export: Download containing the topic distributions for further analysis.

What is it?

Word2Vec maps words into a continuous vector space based on their surrounding contexts in sentences (CBOW architecture). Rather than generating document topics, it captures word-to-word semantic affinity through cosine similarity in a 100-dimensional space.

Parameters

  • key terms: How many core vocabulary words to select as base reference terms for semantic exploration.
  • words: How many of the most contextually similar terms to retrieve for each key term.
  • interaction: How many training passes the model runs through your texts (higher values are slower but can be more precise).
  • clean corpus: Removes common words like "the", "at", "is", as well as numbers and special characters.
  • lemmatize corpus: Simplifies word endings to their root grammatical form (e.g., converts "running" to "run").

Outputs & Visualization

Interactive cards are generated below, each representing a semantic group led by its base term (Group Keyword: "...").

  • Group Keyword: The base word shown at the top with a score of 1.0000 (100%), acting as the reference point for the group.
  • Numerical Value: Context Similarity (0.0 to 1.0) — Measures how often two words appear in the same kinds of sentences. Scores close to 1.0 indicate high contextual affinity.
  • Percentage (%): Semantic Closeness — Direct percentage of connection with the group keyword.
  • Spreadsheet Export: Download containing all vocabulary relationships found.
