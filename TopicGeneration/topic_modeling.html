Modeling Guide

A beginner-friendly guide to choosing a model, setting options, and understanding results.

What is it?

LSA reads all your documents to find words that frequently appear together. It connects these words to form hidden themes, helping you discover the main subjects covered in your files. It achieves this through matrix factorization (SVD) to identify latent patterns.

Parameters

  • topics: How many different themes or subjects you want to discover.
  • words: How many keywords to show inside each theme.
  • interaction: How many calculation passes the model makes (higher values are slower but can be more precise).
  • clean corpus: Removes common words like "the", "at", "is", as well as numbers and special characters.
  • lemmatize corpus: Simplifies word endings to their root grammatical form (e.g., converts "running" to "run").

Outputs & Visualization

Interactive topic cards appear below with progress bars indicating the weight and relevance of each word. You can download the generated lsa_results.xlsx spreadsheet by clicking the download button next to the title.

What is it?

LDA reads all your documents to find words that frequently appear together. It connects these words to form hidden themes, helping you discover the main subjects covered in your files. Unlike LSA, it uses a probabilistic model, assuming documents are mixtures of multiple topics.

Parameters

  • topics: How many different themes or subjects you want to discover.
  • words: How many keywords to show inside each theme.
  • interaction: How many calculation passes the model makes (higher values are slower but can be more precise).
  • clean corpus: Removes common words like "the", "at", "is", as well as numbers and special characters.
  • lemmatize corpus: Simplifies word endings to their root grammatical form (e.g., converts "running" to "run").

What is it?

Word2Vec learns the meaning of words by looking at their neighboring words in sentences. Words used in similar situations are grouped together into visual color-coded clusters.

Parameters

  • clusters: How many color groups or word categories to create.
  • words: How many top words to highlight for each group in the side list.
  • epochs: How many learning cycles the neural network runs through your texts.
  • clean corpus: Removes common words like "the", "at", "is", as well as numbers and special characters.
  • lemmatize corpus: Simplifies word endings to their root grammatical form (e.g., converts "running" to "run").

Outputs & Visualization

An interactive 2D scatter plot where each point is a word (closer points mean closer meanings), alongside color-coded cluster cards. The w2v_results.xlsx spreadsheet is ready to download whenever you want via the download button.
